Professor Language vs Student Language

Course Objective Student Translation
Define common and natural logarithms. I can evaluate log and ln expressions.
Interpret a logarithm as an inverse function. I can move between logarithmic and exponential form.
Use logarithm properties. I can expand and condense logarithms.
Identify domain and range. I can determine allowed x-values and y-values.
Sketch logarithmic graphs. I can graph logs and identify asymptotes.
Analyze growth and decay. I can interpret exponential models.
Find half-life and doubling time. I can calculate and explain them.
Use logarithms to solve equations. I can solve for unknown exponents.
Apply logarithms to pH and sound. I can solve real-world application problems.

Exam Readiness Checklist

If you can confidently answer "yes" to all of the following, you're likely ready for the Chapter 5 exam:

  • □ I can evaluate logarithms.
  • □ I can convert between log and exponential form.
  • □ I can expand logarithms.
  • □ I can condense logarithms.
  • □ I can find domains.
  • □ I can identify ranges.
  • □ I can sketch log graphs.
  • □ I can interpret asymptotes.
  • □ I can solve exponential equations.
  • □ I can find half-life.
  • □ I can find doubling time.
  • □ I can convert between \(ab^t\) and \(ae^{kt}\).
  • □ I can solve pH and decibel problems.
