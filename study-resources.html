Topic-Specific Study Plan

If Log Properties Feel Weak

  • Practice expansion daily
  • Practice condensing daily
  • Memorize product, quotient, and power rules

If Graphing Feels Weak

  • Use Desmos
  • Sketch logs by hand
  • Identify domain and asymptotes
  • Explain graph behavior in words

If Growth Models Feel Weak

  • Practice identifying variables
  • Practice finding rates
  • Practice solving for time
  • Review half-life and doubling time

Self-Learning Strategy

30-Minute Review Session

10 minutes: review notes

10 minutes: practice problems

10 minutes: explain solutions out loud

If you can explain a problem, you probably understand it.

Math Lab Strategy

Before Going

  • Attempt the problem first
  • Circle where you became stuck
  • Bring specific questions
  • Bring calculator and notes

Questions To Ask

  • Can you check my setup?
  • Why did this property apply?
  • Could I solve this another way?
  • How would this appear on an exam?

For a fuller guide, see the Math Lab Companion.

Remember

Most students do not become confident by reading notes repeatedly.

Confidence comes from:

  • Practice
  • Mistakes
  • Corrections
  • Repeated exposure

The goal isn't perfection. The goal is recognizing the process.
